# FAI
It stands for Foregoing AI, though your own personal interpretation is free for you to choose.

A custom programming language & Compiler backend written in pure Rust. An exercise in learning and patience as well as completely foregoing the usage of AI to aid in the developmennt process. Here I will also document my progress in learning the Rust language from scratch and building good git and communication practices to serve as a good reference for any future people to see. Progress will be slow or stagnant for periods of time and I will need to try my best to keep it up.

# Why am I doing this?
As AI is getting better and better at doing many tasks I feel like I have completely lost my touch in the software side of things, constantly relying on it to fix bugs and write code for me.

It has taken most of the fun out of the development process in exchange for lightning fast development, a trait which I find useful in some contexts.

Though the main issue is that I have also started using it for hobby projects: on my github you might see a repo called J-Sharp, a JVM language that was completely coded by Claude Code, mostly as a test of its capabilities, I do plan on actually updating it and making it a somewhat functional product though I want to make something myself completely.

AI is a great tool and I advocate for its use where necessary, but in the realm of things that grant us enjoyment I do feel like taking a step back and trying it ourselves is important.

I am not completely stopping my usage of AI, while there are many issues with its current implementation and development that I won't discuss here the technology in and of itself is something that I as an engineer and programmer cannot simply ignore.

**I want to get the thrill of programming and the excitement of figuring out something new, learning how to think back, make amazing things I myself am more proud of**
